# Phase 4 — Delicacy & Shopping / Recommends & Tips

Read all completed research and original Seven Ninths content specifications. Research and write substantive `research/04_food_shopping_tips.md` with:
- Restaurant & Café & Bar: numerous actual venues across itinerary neighborhoods, local name, cuisine, signature dishes, general price level if supported, nearest station, hours/closures, whether booking is advisable, official link, suitability for coffee/bakery/photography preferences.
- Supermarket & Store & Market: malls, major department stores, specialty shops, local markets, underground malls, independent retail, pertinent opening hours, access, tax-free notes if verified.
- Tourist Travel Pass & Ticket: operator, validity, coverage, conditions, prices dated with sources, comparisons versus pay-as-you-go; list passes only if genuinely useful and verify named pass still exists for the trip dates.

Only recommend geolocatable existing providers; do not claim a restaurant is open or bookable now without live-capable evidence. Append distinct source URLs to the evidence ledger. Avoid generic unsourced top-ten lists.
