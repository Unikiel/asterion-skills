# Around Area — Qualified Same-Day Excursions

**Non-negotiable:** `Around Area` = recommended neighboring towns, cities, sites or geographical areas reachable from the principal trip destination **with a return to the main base on the same calendar day**. It is not a synonym/alias for the main destination. `Around Aera` was a heading typo in earlier brochures, not a different concept.

## Decision and classification

1. Identify the **base**: the actual hotel, main station, or city center designated for the trip. Do not assume the airport or region capital is the base.
2. Identify the candidate's geographic identity and the reason for the excursion (heritage, art, natural scenery, train experience, etc.).
3. Find a plausible **outbound** journey from the base and the **return** route from the candidate to that same base. Determine realistic walk, waiting, interchange and buffer times.
4. Verify there is a meaningful on-site visit window given operating hours, admission windows, daylight where relevant, and traveler constraints.
5. Verify transport home: last practical service, seasonal timetable, and an alternative or safe cut-off. If data is missing, flag verification pending; never claim confirmed day-trip feasibility.
6. Add it under Around Area only when same-day outbound and return is realistic. Otherwise place in a separately labeled `Extensions / Overnight candidates` decision note, not in the published qualified list.

## Default feasibility assessment

No universal mileage threshold: geographical proximity does **not** guarantee easy round trips (islands, mountains, complex transfers), and some distant high-speed rail destinations can still be feasible. Decide by travel time and schedule, not Euclidean distance.

- **Easy**: simple transfers, generous visiting window, reliable return; low walking burden for the requested traveler.
- **Moderate**: more connections or longer transfers, with sufficient visiting time.
- **Demanding**: approximately 2+ hours each direction, low timetable frequency, long walks or tight last-train margin; keep if worthwhile, clearly label and propose backup.
- **Unverified**: missing date-specific hours or transport; do not publish as operationally confirmed.
- **Not a day trip**: cannot safely and realistically return the same day; exclude from Around Area recommendations.

A user's declared time/energy/walking restrictions override default categories. Never infer that a trip is accessible merely because the train ride is short.

## Publication voice

Keep eligibility definitions and calculations in research notes. Use destination-only H3 titles, e.g. `Hirosaki`. Publish concrete outbound/return directions, visit options and practical missed-connection advice; do not repeat "one-day round trip" in every title or explain the skill’s classification policy. The following record is INTERNAL research data, not text to paste into the guide.

## Distinguish neighbouring entries from themed tours

- Main destination: `Kyoto` (hypothetical base), with attractions in Kyoto itself.
- Around Area: `Otsu` or `Uji` **only if** a complete real-world out-and-back visit has been researched and demonstrated feasible for that journey's date and traveler requirements.
- Tours: `Tea heritage route` linking places, possibly inside Kyoto or an Around Area destination. Theme != geographic classification.

**Source precedents (classification examples, not date-specific feasibility claims):** Nagoya brochures separated Toyota and Tokoname; Kobe separated Okayama, Kurashiki and Himeji; Tokyo separated Kamakura, Fujisawa, Hanno and Lake Kawaguchi. Some excursions may require particularly careful feasibility analysis for a specific start point and day.
